# Video script for Gemini (Veo) — "Trusted Trade Agents", max 2:00

How to use this file:
1. Gemini/Veo makes **short clips (about 8 seconds each)**, not one 2-minute film. Generate the **G-shots one by one** with the prompts below, then assemble everything in a video editor (CapCut, iMovie, DaVinci Resolve).
2. **Generate the two character reference images first** (section 1) and attach them to every G-shot prompt, so the chef and the receptionist look the same in every clip.
3. **R-shots are real recordings of our app.** Never ask Gemini to draw the app screens: the judges must see the real system. Gemini only makes the restaurant story around it.
4. Paste each prompt **as is**. Each one repeats the character and style descriptions on purpose: Veo does not remember earlier clips.

Legend: **G** = generated by Gemini/Veo · **R** = real recording (phone screen, laptop screen, or real camera) · **E** = made in the editor.

---

## 1. Style bible (attach to every G-shot)

**Look:** cinematic, natural, warm. Small modern bistro in Helsinki, evening service preparation. Soft warm tungsten light in the dining room, cooler white light in the kitchen. Shallow depth of field, handheld but steady camera, 24 fps, 16:9, realistic (not cartoon, not 3D render).

**Rules for every clip:**
- No readable text, logos, brand names or labels anywhere (packaging, screens, signs, aprons).
- No phone or laptop screen visible facing the camera (screens are added from real recordings).
- No dialogue, no lip-sync. Ambient sound only (kitchen sizzle, quiet dining-room murmur), or silent.
- Same two characters in every clip, same clothes.

**Character A — the chef:** man in his late 30s, short dark brown hair, short trimmed beard, white double-breasted chef jacket with sleeves rolled to the elbow, dark grey apron, calm and focused.

**Character B — the receptionist:** woman in her late 20s, shoulder-length light brown hair tied back, black shirt, small notepad and pen.

**Reference images to generate first (Gemini image generation):**
- *Prompt A:* "Photorealistic portrait, waist up, of a professional chef in his late 30s with short dark brown hair and a short trimmed beard, white double-breasted chef jacket with sleeves rolled to the elbow, dark grey apron, standing in a modern restaurant kitchen with warm and cool light, neutral expression, no logos, no text."
- *Prompt B:* "Photorealistic portrait, waist up, of a restaurant receptionist in her late 20s with shoulder-length light brown hair tied back, black shirt, holding a small notepad and pen, standing at the front desk of a small modern bistro with warm evening light, friendly expression, no logos, no text."

## 3. G-shot prompts (paste one at a time, attach reference images A and B)

### Shot 3 — Handoff in one take (8 s)
> Handheld tracking shot starting at the warm tungsten-lit front desk of a small modern bistro in Helsinki, where a receptionist in her late 20s with shoulder-length light brown hair tied back and a black shirt tears a small handwritten note from her notepad. The camera follows her smoothly through swinging doors into a cooler-lit modern stainless-steel kitchen, where she hands the note to a chef in his late 30s with short dark brown hair, a short trimmed beard, a white double-breasted jacket with rolled sleeves, and a dark grey apron. The chef takes the slip, reads it, and gives a confident nod. Realistic, 24 fps, shallow depth of field. Ambient kitchen sounds: sizzling pan, soft dining-room murmur. ### Shot 9 — Back to cooking (6 s)

A chef in his late 30s with short dark brown hair, a short trimmed beard, a white double-breasted jacket with rolled sleeves and a dark grey apron slides his smartphone back into his apron pocket with a relaxed smile, turns to the stove and tosses pasta in a pan. Camera slowly pulls back. Realistic, 24 fps. Ambient sound: sizzle and pan toss. No visible screen, no readable text, no logos.

### Shot 11 — Served (6 s)

Slow overhead-to-side camera move. Realistic, 24 fps, shallow depth of field. Ambient sound: happy dinner chatter, cutlery. No readable text, no logos, no faces in close-up.

**If a clip comes out wrong:** regenerate with the same prompt; or add at the end "keep the same chef as in the reference image"; or cut the clip shorter in the editor. Do not add text or logos in Veo; add any captions in the editor.

## 4. R-shots: what to record for real (order part, checked live on 2026-11-14)

**Setup (once):** start the 3 local processes from [DEMO-RUN.md](DEMO-RUN.md), reset the gateway data so the queue and audit trail are empty, and open `http://<Mac Wi-Fi IP>:5173/shop.html` on the **real iPhone** (same Wi-Fi as the Mac). The iOS Simulator has no camera, so the scan must be filmed on a real phone.

**The handwritten note** (filmed in shot 4, scanned in shot 6). Write exactly this, clearly, one dish per line:

```
Wednesday dinner, 10 people
Appetizer: bruschette pomodorini mozzarella origano
Fusilli al pesto
Dessert: gelato vaniglia e cioccolato
```

Use **Wednesday**, not "tomorrow": on a weekend "tomorrow" is Sunday, when Valio Aimo does not deliver, and the system then (correctly) refuses every line.
