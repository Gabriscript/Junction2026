# Video script for Gemini (Veo) — "Trusted Trade Agents", max 2:00

How to use this file:
1. Gemini/Veo makes **short clips (about 8 seconds each)**, not one 2-minute film. Generate the **G-shots one by one** with the prompts below, then assemble everything in a video editor (CapCut, iMovie, DaVinci Resolve).
2. **Generate the two character reference images first** (section 1) and attach them to every G-shot prompt, so the chef and the receptionist look the same in every clip.
3. **R-shots are real recordings of our app.** Never ask Gemini to draw the app screens: the judges must see the real system. Gemini only makes the restaurant story around it.
4. Paste each prompt **as is**. Each one repeats the character and style descriptions on purpose: Veo does not remember earlier clips.

Legend: **G** = generated by Gemini/Veo · **R** = real recording (phone screen, laptop screen, or real camera) · **E** = made in the editor.

---

## 1. Style bible (attach to every G-shot)

**Look:** cinematic, natural, warm. Small modern bistro in Helsinki, evening service preparation. Soft warm tungsten light in the dining room, cooler white light in the kitchen. Shallow depth of field, handheld but steady camera, 24 fps, 16:9, realistic (not cartoon, not 3D render).

**Rules for every clip:**
- No readable text, logos, brand names or labels anywhere (packaging, screens, signs, aprons).
- No phone or laptop screen visible facing the camera (screens are added from real recordings).
- No dialogue, no lip-sync. Ambient sound only (kitchen sizzle, quiet dining-room murmur), or silent.
- Same two characters in every clip, same clothes.

**Character A — the chef:** man in his late 30s, short dark brown hair, short trimmed beard, white double-breasted chef jacket with sleeves rolled to the elbow, dark grey apron, calm and focused.

**Character B — the receptionist:** woman in her late 20s, shoulder-length light brown hair tied back, black shirt, small notepad and pen.

**Reference images to generate first (Gemini image generation):**
- *Prompt A:* "Photorealistic portrait, waist up, of a professional chef in his late 30s with short dark brown hair and a short trimmed beard, white double-breasted chef jacket with sleeves rolled to the elbow, dark grey apron, standing in a modern restaurant kitchen with warm and cool light, neutral expression, no logos, no text."
- *Prompt B:* "Photorealistic portrait, waist up, of a restaurant receptionist in her late 20s with shoulder-length light brown hair tied back, black shirt, holding a small notepad and pen, standing at the front desk of a small modern bistro with warm evening light, friendly expression, no logos, no text."

## 3. G-shot prompts (paste one at a time, attach reference images A and B)

### Shot 1 — Establishing (7 s)
> Cinematic establishing shot, early evening, inside a small modern bistro in Helsinki before dinner service. Warm tungsten light, wooden tables being set with glasses and napkins, large windows with blue dusk outside. Slow dolly forward from the entrance towards the open kitchen at the back, where a chef in a white double-breasted jacket with sleeves rolled up and a dark grey apron works at the pass, slightly out of focus. Realistic, 24 fps, shallow depth of field. Ambient sound: quiet clinking glasses, distant kitchen sizzle. No people facing camera, no readable text, no logos, no signs.

### Shot 2 — The booking (8 s)
> Medium close-up at the front desk of a small modern bistro, warm evening light. A receptionist in her late 20s with shoulder-length light brown hair tied back and a black shirt holds a desk phone to her ear with one shoulder, nods, and writes several lines on a small paper notepad with a pen. Camera slowly pushes in on her hands writing; the handwriting is not readable (soft focus). Realistic, 24 fps, shallow depth of field. Ambient sound: soft dining-room murmur. No dialogue, no lip-sync, no readable text, no logos.

### Shot 3 — Into the kitchen (8 s)
> Handheld tracking shot following a receptionist in her late 20s with light brown hair tied back and a black shirt as she walks through swinging doors into a busy modern restaurant kitchen with cool white light and steel counters. She tears a small handwritten note from her notepad and hands it to a chef in his late 30s with short dark brown hair, a short trimmed beard, a white double-breasted jacket with rolled sleeves and a dark grey apron, who is chopping herbs. He takes the note, reads it, and gives a small confident nod. Realistic, 24 fps. Ambient kitchen sounds: sizzling pan, knife on board. No dialogue, no lip-sync, the note's text is not readable, no logos.

### Shot 5 — Phone out (6 s)
> Close-up in a modern restaurant kitchen with cool white light. A chef in a white double-breasted jacket with rolled sleeves and a dark grey apron wipes his hands on a kitchen towel tucked in his apron, places a small handwritten note on the steel counter, and takes a smartphone out of his apron pocket. He looks down at the phone; the screen faces away from the camera and is not visible. Realistic, 24 fps, shallow depth of field. Ambient kitchen sound. No readable text, no logos, no visible screen.

### Shot 9 — Back to cooking (6 s)
> Medium shot in a modern restaurant kitchen with cool white light and steam rising from pans. A chef in his late 30s with short dark brown hair, a short trimmed beard, a white double-breasted jacket with rolled sleeves and a dark grey apron slides his smartphone back into his apron pocket with a relaxed smile, turns to the stove and tosses pasta in a pan. Camera slowly pulls back. Realistic, 24 fps. Ambient sound: sizzle and pan toss. No visible screen, no readable text, no logos.

### Shot 11 — Served (6 s)
> Warm cinematic shot the next evening in a small modern bistro in Helsinki, tungsten light, candles. A long table of ten guests laughing and talking while plates are served: bruschette with chopped tomatoes, mozzarella and oregano; bowls of fusilli with green basil pesto and grated parmesan; small bowls with one scoop of vanilla and one scoop of chocolate ice cream. Slow overhead-to-side camera move. Realistic, 24 fps, shallow depth of field. Ambient sound: happy dinner chatter, cutlery. No readable text, no logos, no faces in close-up.

**If a clip comes out wrong:** regenerate with the same prompt; or add at the end "keep the same chef as in the reference image"; or cut the clip shorter in the editor. Do not add text or logos in Veo; add any captions in the editor.
